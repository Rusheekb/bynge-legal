# Bynge Privacy Policy

**Effective October 14, 2026**

Bynge is a free app for ranking the films and TV shows you watch and sharing your taste with friends. It is operated by an individual developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Bynge collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

## Information we collect

**Account information.** Your email address and password (stored securely as a one-way hash, which we can't read) to create and sign in to your account.

**Profile information.** Your username, and optionally a display name, bio, and profile photo.

**Content you create.** The titles you log, mark as watched, or add to your watchlist; your rankings and scores; the head-to-head comparisons you make; notes and reviews you write; comments; reactions; your diary; and the accounts you follow or block.

**Reports.** If you report an account or content, we store the report, the reason you chose, and what was reported.

**Notification token.** If you allow notifications, your device's push notification token, so we can deliver notifications you've turned on. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings or in Bynge.

**Letterboxd imports.** If you import from Letterboxd, the export file is read on your device. Only the matched titles, years, and ratings are saved to your account; the file itself is not uploaded.

**Anonymous usage and diagnostics.** Unless you turn off Settings → Share Anonymous Usage Data, Bynge sends anonymous events such as "a ranking was completed" or "the app crashed", with coarse details like whether it was a film or a TV show. These events never include the titles you rank, your notes, your username, email, or account. They're tied only to a one-way hash of a random device identifier, so they can't be connected to you or your account.

**Information we don't collect.** We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history, advertising identifier, or any data for advertising. Bynge contains no advertising SDKs.

## How we use information

- To run your account and the app's features: ranking, your lists, your profile, following friends, and the activity feed.
- To show your public profile and, unless your account is private, your activity to other signed-in Bynge users.
- To send notifications you've turned on, such as new followers and friend activity.
- To keep Bynge safe: enforcing our Terms, reviewing reports, and blocking abusive accounts.
- To respond when you contact us.

We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## What other people can see

Your **username, display name, profile photo, and bio** are visible to other signed-in Bynge users, as are the accounts you follow.

By default, your **rankings, watch activity, diary, reviews, and reactions** are visible to other signed-in users. If you turn on **Private Account** in Settings, people have to request to follow you, and only the followers you approve can see them. Follow requests are visible only to you and the person who sent them.

Comments you post are visible to everyone who can see the post you commented on. The post's owner can delete comments on their post.

People you have blocked can't see your activity or comments, and you won't see theirs.

**Bynge scores and the leaderboard.** If your account is public, your scores count toward each title's Bynge score. It's shown only as an average, and only once at least three people have ranked the title, never with your name. Public accounts also appear on the leaderboard, which shows how many titles you've ranked, unless you turn off Settings → Show on Leaderboard. Private accounts never count toward Bynge scores, and appear on the leaderboard only to followers they've approved (and only if they haven't turned it off).

## Who we share information with

We use a small number of service providers to run Bynge:

- **Supabase**, which hosts Bynge's database, sign-in, and file storage, on servers in the United States.
- **Apple Push Notification service**, which delivers notifications to your device.
- **TelemetryDeck**, a privacy-focused analytics service, which receives only the anonymous usage and diagnostics events described above.
- **The Movie Database (TMDB)**, which supplies film and TV information and artwork. Requests to TMDB contain only title identifiers and search text, never your account or personal information.

These providers process data only to provide their services to Bynge.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the safety of our users or the public, or to enforce our Terms.

## How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, rankings, logs, notes, reactions, follows, reports you've made, notification token, and profile photo are permanently deleted from our live systems immediately. Residual copies in our hosting provider's backups are removed within 30 days.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access and export.** You can export your rankings as a CSV file from Settings → Export Rankings. To request a copy of all your data, email us.
- **Correction.** You can edit your profile at any time.
- **Deletion.** You can delete your account and all associated data in Settings → Delete Account, or by emailing us.
- **Notifications.** You can turn notifications off in Settings or in iOS Settings.
- **Privacy.** You can make your account private in Settings.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the European Economic Area), you may have additional rights, such as the right to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any right, email us at the address below. We will respond within 30 days.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. Your sign-in session is stored in the iOS Keychain. Access to data is restricted at the database level, so each user can change only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Children

Bynge is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll notify you in the app before they take effect. The effective date above shows when the policy last changed.
