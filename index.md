# Bynge Support

Bynge lets you rank the films and TV shows you watch through quick head-to-head comparisons, and share your taste with friends.

## Contact

Email **support@byngeapp.com**. We usually reply within 2 business days, and we handle reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.

## Common questions

**How does ranking work?**
When you log a title, Bynge asks how you felt about it, then shows you a few head-to-head matchups against titles already on your list. Each choice narrows down where the new title belongs, so it usually takes only a handful of comparisons. Scores run from 10.0 for your top title down to 1.0.

**I forgot my password.**
On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?** and enter your email. We'll send you a link to set a new password.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to **Profile → Settings → Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your profile, rankings, notes, and all other data. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

**How do I report or block someone?**
Open the person's profile and tap the **⋯** button, then choose **Report** or **Block**. You can also report an individual post from the feed. Blocked accounts no longer appear in your feed.

**What does a private account do?**
With **Private Account** on (Settings → Privacy & Safety), your rankings, activity, and notes are visible only to you and the people you follow.

**Can I import from Letterboxd?**
Yes. Export your data from Letterboxd, then go to **Settings → Import from Letterboxd** and choose the export file.

**How do I export my rankings?**
Go to **Settings → Export Rankings as CSV**.
